RED TAG RUMBLE

RED TAG RUMBLE

Fourteen days on the shelf, then it is free or it is trash. Build a bot from other people's abandoned projects and drive it into another one. One to four gamepads, split screen.

Controls

Pads and keys

Pads
Plug in up to four. Left stick drives, A or the right trigger is the weapon, B brakes or rights you, X taunts, L boosts, Start pauses.
Keyboard
Pad 1. Arrows drive (no stick needed), Z weapon, X brake, A taunt, S hold for the controls card, Q boost, Enter is start: ready and go in the lobby, lock and go in the garage, pause in the fight.
Garage
Up and down pick a row, left and right cycle a part, A (Z) locks, Start (Enter) locks and starts the fight, a tap of Y (S) cycles the presets, X (A) rolls a random bot. In the arena vote the stick picks and A votes.
The rules

Twenty tags

Every part on the shelf is red tagged. Spend twenty tags on a chassis, a drive, a weapon and armour. Hit the side, not the front. Under 30 HP you hit harder for eight seconds. Dropped panels are HP for anyone who drives over them. If nobody hits anyone for twelve seconds, Operations clears the aisle.

A wasmcart cart: three.c for the 3D, Bellows for the sound. The same .wasc runs here, in RetroArch, and on the shop's game station.
